## Panels
- **Event feed:** events appear on the replay clock with an action chip coloured by action. Interrupts show the 🔔 icon. Batched messages from people show the auto-reply inline. Clicking an event selects it.
- **Snapshot + why:** for the selected event, the exact state JSON sent to Jev, bars for each Jev answer, the policy row that fired, and what the LLM baseline chose.
- **Phone:** only interrupts arrive here. A CI regression interrupt opens the LLM explanation. Evening mode dims the phone.
- **Metrics strip:** live from `metrics.js` (08), recomputed when settings change.

## Controls
- Play, pause, **jump to next demo moment**, and 1x/10x speed. The day runs 08:00–20:00. At 1x one replay minute takes 1 real second (12 minutes for the whole day); 10x takes 72 seconds. "Jump" fast-forwards to 1 replay minute before the next `demo_moment` event and pauses.
- The budget dots and counters reset at 08:00 and are recomputed from the start of the day whenever settings change.
- **Urgency** (the 1–5 Score) shows as a small badge on each message. It's for display only.
- **Settings drawer** (04): changes re-route the day so far and the metrics instantly.
- **Sender view toggle:** the feed is swapped for the senders' side: each batched person sees the auto-reply and a "truly urgent" button (scripted re-route).
- "Ask Sanad" modal (04).
- Digest overlay at the end of the replay (05).

Visual direction is the builder's call: dark, calm and legible on a projector, with one accent colour per action.
